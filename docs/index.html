Regime-Aware GBWM03 / 14

People don't invest for returns.
They invest to arrive somewhere.

Retirement. A house. A child's education. A normal investor never says "maximize my Sharpe ratio." They say "don't fail my goal." So the objective is the probability of reaching it — not raw return.

Analogy
A trading bot drives fast.
A goal-based agent is GPS — it arrives.

The contribution

A strategy shouldn't drive the same way
in every market weather.

Regime probabilities enter the state via an HMM belief — so the policy stops being stationary.

Bull
growth · risk-on
P̂ 0.42
Leans into equities — near 100%. The trend rewards risk.
Bear
decline · defensive
P̂ 0.18
Hedges down to ~20%. Capital preservation over chasing return.
High-vol
unstable · risk-off
P̂ 0.21
Reduces turnover. Avoids decisions amplified by noise.
Stable
normal · balanced
P̂ 0.19
Rebalances toward the long-term glide path.

Regime-Aware GBWM09 / 14

Trading bots look exciting.
They are weak projects.

Trading bot

"Make more money."

Drives as fast as possible.
  • Easy to overfit history
  • "Maximize return" is a weak objective
  • Lucky backtests aren't proof
Goal-based RL

"Reach the goal safely."

Recalculates the route.
  • Clear, falsifiable objective
  • Honest metric: probability of success
  • A defendable claim, not a backtest

When regimes earn their keep

On easy goals, timing adds little. On hard goals, it wins.

If the goal is easy, "gamble when behind, coast when ahead" already nearly maxes out — so a regime-blind agent is enough. Push the target higher and the regime-aware agent pulls ahead.

Ambitious goal $550k  ·  Glide 0.17 → G-Learner 0.23 → Regime-aware 0.24
…with lower shortfall and lower drawdown.
